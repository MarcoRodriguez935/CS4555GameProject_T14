**Game Name:** Catacomb Crew

**Team Name:** Launchpad Productions

**Team Members:** Marco Rodriguez, Ryan Garcia, Fahed Taher

**Logline:**
Two explorers find themselves trapped in a dark, ancient catacomb and must work together to explore, gather tools, and complete puzzles, all while avoiding the monsters that lurk deep within.

**Gameplay:**
Catacomb Crew is planned as a survival-horror dungeon crawler in a top-down/over-the-shoulder perspective. Designed for two players to play on a single device using two sides of a keyboard, the game focuses on teamwork and exploration. Players are tasked with solving puzzles and searching for an exit, all while avoiding monsters with unique abilities and behaviors. Monsters cannot be defeated directly; instead, the players must evade and avoid them. Visibility is restricted to a small radius, forcing the players to move cautiously and rely on each other and their tools to stay aware of their surroundings. Puzzles range from pulling levers in specific combinations, igniting several torches placed around a level, or locating a mysterious object with dark powers. As the players progress, they must avoid an increasing amount of monsters and navigate new regions. Each region will introduce the player to new layouts, challenges, and items, allowing the players to come up with unique strategies.

**Technology:**
The most significant technical feature will be designing and implementing the enemy AI. Monsters will have unique abilities and behaviors, allowing them to “hear” and react to player movement, item/environmental interactions, and other sounds. This system will encourage the players to think carefully about their movement and item usage to create distractions and navigate their environment. The game will include a dynamic camera that provides a top-down and over-the-shoulder view to follow each player as they explore. Other features include traps within levels and a fog-of-war map revealed as the players explore.

**Story:**
When two friends explore a forgotten catacomb, the entrance collapses, leaving them in darkness. As the dust settles, they hear an eerie screech and the rattle of chains echoing from the corridors, forcing them to press onward. As they search for an escape, they encounter remnants of past explorers, ancient mechanisms, and strange artifacts. Together, they must solve puzzles, navigate narrow corridors, and outmaneuver the horrors that stalk below.
